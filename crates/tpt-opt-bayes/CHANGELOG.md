## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Initial scaffold generated by `cargo xtask new-crate`.
- Gaussian-process Bayesian optimiser with a Matérn 5/2 kernel: `BayesOpt`
  (`ask` / `tell` / `best`) and the one-shot `minimise` driver.
- Hyperparameters fitted by maximising the marginal likelihood with
  `tpt_opt_core::unconstrained::lbfgs`; refitted as the data grows.
- Expected-improvement acquisition, seeded and deterministic.
